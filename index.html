ABOUT THE ATLAS

A visual map of the grammar learned by RiboGRAM.

RiboGRAM Atlas organizes 92,781 sequences from all 4,178 families in the Rfam 15.0 Seed collection. Positions are derived from a two-dimensional UMAP projection of RiboGRAM sequence representations.
